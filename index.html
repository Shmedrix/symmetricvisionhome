How did I get here?

"Inducing Flashbacks since 2014"

Hi! I'm Symmetric Vision, and ever since I can remember myself I've been consumed by the concept of our perception. It fascinates me how we perceive the World around us, and the many different ways we can tune our perception - meditation, dreaming, sleep deprivation, mind-altering substances, breathing techniques, etc. I got so captivated by this idea, that I started replicating these altered states (it's all subjective, and we all know that), so people could catch a glimpse at what it's like to perceive the World through a new set of eyes.
