"Inducing Flashback since 2014"

My name is Raimonds, I am also known as Symmetric Vision, and ever since I can remember myself, I have been consumed by the concept of our perception.

It fascinates me how we perceive the world around us, and the many different ways we can tune our perception: meditation, dreaming, sleep deprivation, mind-altering substances, breathing techniques, and more.

I became captivated by this idea and started replicating altered states, so people could catch a glimpse at what it is like to perceive the world through a new set of eyes.
